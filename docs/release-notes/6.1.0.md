## Before you upgrade

Nothing changes unless you turn something on. Your settings, lists and services carry over, the helper stays the same, and no new prompt appears on the first launch.

## What's new

- **Per-app routing, off by default.** Until now VPN Bypass routed destinations, so a rule for an app did nothing. Turn on Settings > General > Per-app routing (or `vpnb engine.on`), and the apps you list reach the internet around the VPN whatever address they connect to: in Bypass mode from the new Apps list, in Custom mode from a rule for an app with the Direct route. It takes their TCP connections to public IPv4 addresses and nothing else. Other apps, IPv6, UDP, local addresses and the VPN client's own traffic keep their route, and domains, services and IP ranges keep working through the routes for every app. https://geiserx.github.io/VPN-Bypass/how-it-works/#per-app-routing
- **macOS asks twice when you turn it on.** First allow the extension in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Network Extensions, with your administrator password; then allow VPN Bypass to add a proxy configuration. Say no and nothing else changes. VPN Bypass must be in the Applications folder. A Mac managed by an administrator may not allow it; Settings > General then says it is blocked.
- **When something goes wrong, the listed apps use the VPN.** If VPN Bypass quits, within 2 minutes. If the extension crashes, new connections wait about 5 seconds once, the app starts it again, and a second crash within 10 minutes turns it off.
- **Turning it off is the old way.** The switch removes the extension and its configuration (macOS asks for your administrator password), and the app works as 6.0 did. Turn it off before `brew uninstall`: Homebrew cannot remove a system extension. https://geiserx.github.io/VPN-Bypass/troubleshooting/#remove-the-per-app-routing-extension
- **`vpnb engine.status`, `engine.on`, `engine.off`, `engine.remove`** and **`vpnb app.list`, `app.add`, `app.rm`** do the same from a script. https://geiserx.github.io/VPN-Bypass/usage/#command-line-control-vpnb
