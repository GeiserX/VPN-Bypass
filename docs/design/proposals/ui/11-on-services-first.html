11The services that are on come first

The Services page lists all 37 built-in services in one fixed order, and the four that are on are spread through it: Telegram, WhatsApp and YouTube near the top, the fourth further down. To answer "what am I bypassing?" the user scrolls the whole list or reads the count. The proposal puts an On section at the top with the enabled services, then the rest in the usual order. Turning a service off moves it down on the next visit, not while the pointer is on it.
