10Verify Routes says what it checked, and failures come first

Verify pings at most 10 destinations: single addresses only, never ranges, always the first 10 in sort order. Its card shows a count such as "9/10" under a badge that says 62 routes, and lists three results picked from a Swift dictionary, whose order is arbitrary. So "10/10" reads as everything checked, and a failure can sit in the seven rows nobody sees. The proposal says what was checked, lists failures first and links to the full result in Logs.
