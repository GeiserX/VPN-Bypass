13A Status page answers "is it working right now?" in one place

Today the answer is spread across two pages. Settings > General mixes live state into a long page of settings: the helper, the most common reason nothing works, is card three, the DNS schedule sits inside a settings card, and Network Status and the tunnel diagnostics are at the bottom. Route counts, and the DNS schedule a second time, live in a Route Health block on the Logs page. The proposal adds a Status page as the first item in the toolbar. It gathers the helper, the VPN and gateway, the routes, DNS, the tunnels and recent warnings. General keeps settings only and Logs keeps the log.
