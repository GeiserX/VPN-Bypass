5Adding a domain says what happened

The add field in the dropdown and on the Domains tab always empties after Return. A duplicate, an empty result after cleaning, or a DNS failure only writes a log line. Worse, typing 10.0.0.0/24 on the Bypass list silently becomes the single host 10.0.0.0, because the cleaner cuts everything after the slash as if it were a URL path. The proposal keeps the text when the add fails, says why in one line under the field, and names what was actually saved when the input was rewritten.
