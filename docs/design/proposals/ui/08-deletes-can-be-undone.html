8Deletes and All/None can be taken back

Every trash icon deletes on the first click, with nothing to undo. Deleting a custom service takes its whole domain list with it. All turns on 37 services at once, Netflix and Steam included, so all their traffic leaves the VPN. None is red, like a delete, though it only turns things off. The proposal keeps delete at one click but leaves an Undo line in the list, with ⌘Z. All and None move into a small menu, and turning on many services at once asks first.
