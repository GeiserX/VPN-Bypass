9Switches with names, and keyboard shortcuts

Every row switch is an unlabelled Toggle("") shrunk with scaleEffect, so VoiceOver reads 37 identical "switch, on" on the Services page, and the drawn switch is smaller than the space it takes. Icon-only buttons have no label of their own, and the app has no keyboard shortcut at all. The proposal names every switch and icon button for VoiceOver, uses the native small switch, and adds ⌘R (Refresh), ⌘, (Settings) and ⌘Q (Quit) in the dropdown and ⌘F (search) on Services, listed where macOS lists shortcuts.
