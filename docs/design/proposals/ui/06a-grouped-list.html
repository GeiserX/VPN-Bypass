6AThe question as a grouped list, with the Services page's own switches

The tinted blue box goes. The question becomes a plain section title over two grouped lists that look like System Settings: six common services, each with the symbol the dropdown already draws in its service chips, and one row to add a site. The switch is the one the Services page uses, so a switch flipped here shows up flipped there, and it applies at once. Trade-off: the most familiar and the most direct, but the fresh-install dropdown grows from 372 to about 520 pt (the normal view is 556), and Zoom and Teams need two new symbols in the chip map (they draw a globe today). Recommended.
