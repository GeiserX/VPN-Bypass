16"Route" means one thing, and the Bypass list stops sounding like Custom mode

Today "route" names three things: the kernel entries the app installs (the dropdown's Active Routes, "62 routes"), the ways out in Custom mode (Direct, a VPN, a proxy, a Tailscale peer), and the ways out that have rules (Routes In Use). A user who reads "62 routes" in the dropdown and "1/1 active" on the Routes page is counting two different things under one word. The Bypass list is titled Custom Domains, although Custom is also the name of a mode. The proposal keeps "route" for the Custom-mode ways out, which the socket verbs route.* already use, calls the kernel entries addresses, and names each list after its mode. Only on-screen words change; the socket verbs keep their names.

Route: a way out for traffic. Direct, your VPN, a proxy or a Tailscale peer. Address: one IP address or range the app has sent a certain way. Telegram is 8 of them.
